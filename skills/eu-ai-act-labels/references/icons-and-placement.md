# The EU icons and where labels go

Published by the European Commission with the Code of Practice (Annex I), 10 June 2026:
https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/eu-icons-labelling-ai-generated-content

## The three icons

| Icon | Shape | Use |
|------|-------|-----|
| Basic | round, "AI" | when AI was involved in a deepfake or published text; with a custom text label ("Stimmen mit [AI] erzeugt") or an interactive second layer; where space is short |
| AI GENERATED | pill, "AI GENERATED" | content fully generated by AI, no human-made elements or editorial control apart from prompting |
| AI MODIFIED | pill, "AI MODIFIED" | existing human-made content partly altered with AI (e.g. a face swapped in an authentic photo) |

Four colour variants each: black, white, black at 50 % opacity, white at 50 % opacity. SVG and PNG.
The main element is the capitalised English "AI"; a national acronym ("KI") is allowed only where English
is legally incompatible with language rules. Only "AI" is mandatory; GENERATED/MODIFIED are encouraged.

## Terms

- Free to use, no attribution to the Commission or the AI Office required.
- Optional: an equivalent label that meets the design and placement rules is acceptable too.
- Using the icon does not by itself establish compliance; the deployer stays responsible.
- Non-signatories of the Code who use the icons do not signal adherence to it.

## Placement and design rules (Code, Section 2; Commission icon page)

- Clearly perceivable and distinguishable **at the latest at first exposure**, without interaction.
- Embedded **directly in the content** where possible (a corner of the image), or an equivalent overlay
  in the interface; it should stay when content is reshared or downloaded where feasible.
- Not obscured by other overlay elements; not covering text that is part of the content.
- A clearly visible size (drafts named 24 × 24 px for digital as a minimum; keep the icon legible).
- Video: in a corner for the whole duration when the video is wholly or mostly AI; during AI segments
  otherwise; short videos (under a minute) for the whole duration.
- Audio: the icon in the track or episode artwork and next to the player where there is a visual
  interface.
- Text: next to the AI text (at the top of an article, beside a summary box).
- If a disclosure appears only briefly, it must stay visible long enough to be read.

## Accessibility

- Alt text or ARIA label so assistive technology identifies the content as AI-generated or manipulated –
  e.g. the icon element with `role="img"` and `aria-label="KI-generiert: Dieses Bild wurde mit KI erzeugt."`.
- Do not overwrite the image's own alt text with the notice.
- The second layer (details on hover, focus or click) must be reachable with assistive technologies.

## Wording

- Plain language, no jargon, no abbreviations other than "AI"/"KI".
- User testing for the icons: comprehension improved when the basic icon was paired with a text label
  such as "modified".
- German: „KI-generiert“ · „Mit KI verändert“ · „Dieses Bild wurde mit KI erzeugt.“ · „Dieses Bild wurde
  mit KI verändert.“ · „Dieses Video wurde mit KI erzeugt.“ · „Diese Aufnahme wurde mit KI erzeugt.“
- English: "AI-generated" · "Modified with AI" · "This image was created with AI." · "This image was
  modified with AI."
